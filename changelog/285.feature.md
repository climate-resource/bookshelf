Every SDK error can now be imported from `bookshelf`.
The newly exported errors are:

- `RequestValidationError`
- `AuthConfigurationError`
- `ActionsTokenError`
- `OAuthError`
- `OAuthProtocolError`
- `EntryNotFoundError`
- `SelectionError`
- `InvalidBundleError`
- `InvalidRecipeError`
- `InvalidReferenceError`
