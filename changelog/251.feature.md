Added recorded placements.
A recorded build can now attach a resource its organisation already holds to its book under any unused name,
by passing `book.attach` a handle from `build.use()`, a `bookshelf://` reference or a tracking id.
Replay and previews send the placed resource's `tracking_id`, and a placement adds no lineage.
The bundle schema moved to 3.8, and `bookshelf validate` now lists placements.
