Gave `bookshelf://` references and CLI addresses one edition rule.
An edition is written `_eNNN` in ASCII digits, padded to three and starting at `_e001`,
so a reference spelt `_e2`, `_e0002` or `_e000` is now refused.
References also refuse dot segments, `@`, `?`, `#` and whitespace.

Renamed the `search --facets` key `licences` to `licenses`, matching the `latest_license` field.
