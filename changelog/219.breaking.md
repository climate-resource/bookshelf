Removes `Bookshelf.list_books()` and `AsyncBookshelf.list_books()`, because `bs.volume(name)`
answers what they were reached for and reads one endpoint rather than paging `/v1/books`.
Use `volume.versions`, `volume.latest` and `volume.editions(version)` in their place.
Listing draft books has no facade equivalent, so reach for `BookshelfClient.list_books`
or the `bookshelf show` command instead.
