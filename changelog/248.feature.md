Exposes what a publisher tool otherwise copies from the SDK's private modules.

- `bookshelf.STAGING_API_URL` and `bookshelf.PRODUCTION_API_URL` name the two deployments.
- `Bookshelf.get_or_create_volume` returns the volume and whether this call created it,
  treating a 409 from a concurrent creator as already present.
- `APIError` and its typed subclasses, such as `NotFoundError` and `ConflictError`,
  are exported from `bookshelf`.
