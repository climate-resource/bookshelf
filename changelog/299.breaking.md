`bookshelf record` now refuses a pointer the platform would refuse, at `register_external` and at recipe load,
instead of writing a bundle that `validate` then refused.
A recipe `uri` input must be a public `https` URL, so a feedstock fetching over `http://` needs updating.
The pointer check also reads `localhost`, `127.1`, `0x7f000001` and other numeric host forms as the address they reach.
