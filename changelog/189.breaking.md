Replaces the SDK's `logical_key` with `name`.

- `logical_key=` becomes `name=` on `Used`, `RegisterItem`, `register`, `register_many`,
  `register_external` and the recording adapters.
- `Used(name=...)` now resolves only against the resources registered by the same request.
  The platform dropped its org-wide fallback to the most recent resource sharing a key,
  so a resource from an earlier build has to be referenced by its tracking id.
- `synthesise_pointer_hash` drops the key from its seed to match the backend,
  which hashes `{type, sorted(locations)}` only.
  A hashless external pointer registered by an older SDK recorded a hash the backend
  would not have agreed with.
- `GET /v1/resources` trades its `logical_key` filter for `volume`, `name` and `hash`.
- Bumps `BUNDLE_SCHEMA_VERSION` to `2.0`.
