Adds the `bookshelf://` scheme to a recipe's `resources:`, so a feedstock can build on a published book.

- A resource written as `uri: bookshelf://primap-hist/v2.7_e002/by_country` resolves to the resource
  the platform already holds, and `build.use` hands back its existing tracking id.
  Nothing is fetched from upstream and nothing new is catalogued, so `used=` cites the original.
- The entry may be left off where the book holds exactly one, and the edition may be left off to take
  the newest.
- A `bookshelf://` resource states no `sha256`, because the platform is where that digest comes from.
  It may leave `type` out for the same reason, and a stated type is checked against the resource.
