Removed agent identities from the SDK and CLI.
They may come back after 1.0.

- `bookshelf auth login` no longer takes `--agent`, `--claim` or `--email`.
- `bookshelf auth logout` no longer takes `--no-revoke`, and its JSON is now `{"cleared": [...]}`.
- `bookshelf auth list --json` reports `default` in place of `active`, and no longer reports `kind`, `claimed` or `assertion_expires_at`.
- `bookshelf auth switch --json` no longer reports `kind`.
- `bookshelf auth whoami` no longer reports `claimed` or an `agent` kind.
- `bookshelf.auth.BsatAssertion` and `bookshelf.OAuthProtocolError` were removed.
- Agent logins stored by an earlier release candidate are ignored, and `bookshelf auth logout` clears them.
