Retries no longer amplify a platform outage.

- A data read (`/data`, timeseries, facets, preview and schema) is no longer retried after a 5xx or a read timeout.
- A 500 and a connect timeout are no longer retried, and connecting now times out after 10 seconds.
- Retries back off with jitter, waiting at least half a second before the first replay.
- A non-JSON 4xx error page, such as one from Cloudflare, now raises `GatewayError` instead of `UnexpectedResponseError` or a status-specific error.
