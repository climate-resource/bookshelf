Looking up a missing entry with `book["name"]` now raises `EntryNotFoundError`,
and filtering on an unknown column raises `SelectionError`.
Both are still `KeyError` subclasses.
`NotFoundError` is now also a `LookupError`.
`Bundle.read` now raises `InvalidBundleError`, which is also a `ValueError`,
for invalid YAML, a bad `schema_version` or a manifest the schema rejects.
