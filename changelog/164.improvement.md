Splits `bookshelf.publisher.record` into the driver, the recording adapter, and the recipe.

- Moves `RecordedResource`, `RecordingActivity`, `RecordedDraftBook`, `RecordingSink` and
  `RecordingBookshelf` to `bookshelf.publisher.recording`,
  with the serialisation helpers they use.
- Moves `RecordRecipe` and `load_record_recipe` to `bookshelf.publisher.recipe`.
- Leaves `setup`, `SetupResult`, `run_record` and `parse_parameters` in `bookshelf.publisher.record`,
  which drops from 971 lines to 229.
- Names the composed book visibility precedence rule `resolve_book_visibility`,
  gives it one home in `bookshelf.publisher.recipe`,
  and tests it directly.
  The behaviour is unchanged.

The public interface is unchanged.
Every name exported from `bookshelf.publisher` imports from the same path and behaves the same.
