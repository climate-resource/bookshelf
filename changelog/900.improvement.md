Generates the client's 33 mechanical operation method pairs from the `build_*` and `parse_*` pairs in `_core/ops.py`.

- `scripts/generate_client.py` emits the region below the marker in `_core/client.py`. CI regenerates and diffs it, so the sync and async halves of the client can no longer drift.
- The generator asserts that every `OP_REGISTRY` operation reaches a client method, so a new API operation cannot be silently left unexposed.
- Adds a signature-level parity test over the facades and the consume resources, which generation does not reach. It compares shapes, not behaviour.
