Removes the retired recipe and lock surface from `bookshelf.publisher`.
These names implemented the ADR-0007 recipe-to-lock publish path,
which ADR 0011 replaced with record then replay.

- `Recipe`, `RecipeBook` and `load_recipe` are replaced by `RecordRecipe` and `load_record_recipe`.
- `AggregateLock`, `build_lock`, `build_aggregate_lock`, `mask_lock` and `mask_aggregate_lock` are replaced by
  the bundle manifest, which `Bundle` writes as `manifest.lock`.
- `bookshelf.publisher.notebook.execute_notebook` and `prepare_notebook_items` are replaced by
  `execute_python_build`, which runs a standalone Python build file.
- `bookshelf.publisher._models` is removed. Its shapes are available from the generated models.
