Kept the 0.4 consumer API working on top of the platform, with a `DeprecationWarning` at every call.

- `bookshelf.BookShelf` and `bookshelf.LocalBook` still resolve, and live in `bookshelf.legacy` alongside the old `UnknownBook`, `UnknownVersion` and `UnknownEdition` errors.
- `BOOKSHELF_CACHE_LOCATION` is honoured as a fallback for `BOOKSHELF_CACHE_DIR`, and `BOOKSHELF_REMOTE` or a `remote_bookshelf` URL now warns instead of being silently ignored.
- `as_long_df(legacy_columns=True)` reproduces the 0.4 long format.
