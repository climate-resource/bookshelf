Bakes the editorial metadata a recipe resolves onto each book at publish,
so publishing a new version no longer rewrites what every earlier version says about itself.

- The eighteen baked fields reach the draft call,
  fifteen inside the API's nested `discovery` object
  and `description`, `license` and `authors` beside it.
- The bundle records the resolved values, so replay sends what was recorded.
- The recipe's `release_date` is mapped to the API's `source_release_date` at the boundary.
- Removes `citation` from the volume surface, which the platform moved onto the book.
